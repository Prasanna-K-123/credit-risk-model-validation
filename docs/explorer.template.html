What the validation concludes

Research use only

Model selection, calibration and policy selection used separate, purged periods. That protects the comparison from using final outcomes to choose the winner. It does not establish prospective availability, a stable future regime, or the value of an intervention.

The calibrated booster improves pooled Brier loss against calibrated logistic regression. The illustrative cost difference has a bank-bootstrap interval that crosses zero; annual-cohort resampling tells a different story. Both are reported.

Explicit review triggers

Added after the original study as transparent review examples. PSI and recall thresholds are illustrative, not regulatory standards or a newly preregistered decision rule. Missing evidence prevents a passing review.

Each December report is assumed usable the next April 1. The following one-year outcome horizon is separated by purged years at role boundaries. Repeated bank certificates across time are intentional; this is not unseen-bank validation.

Data, limitations and execution evidence

237,253 eligible bank-year observations are rebuilt from 29 annual financial extracts plus institution and failure records. Public frozen source objects and the full prediction file are checksum-verified before reuse. Seven registered fits, two monotone calibrators, policy thresholds, paired bank/year bootstrap and the all-case audit are actually replayed.

Current historical source revisions are not original information vintages. Three-month report availability is assumed. Failure with competing nonfailure exits is not borrower PD, LGD, expected credit loss or IFRS 9 compliance. Rare events, annual cadence, nominal bank-size features and dependence across banks/years limit interpretation. No realized savings, bank deployment or regulatory endorsement is claimed.

Original source, protocol and historical results remain frozen. The independent review added on 28 October 2026 checks metrics, every cohort, all failed-bank cases and feature drift. The execution receipt records the actual run and environment.
