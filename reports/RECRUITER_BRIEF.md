# Credit-risk project: concise explanation

Built a chronological model-validation study over 300,000 Freddie Mac loans and 20.15 million monthly records. Audited six source vintages, constructed leakage-controlled SQL snapshots and explicitly censored 12-month labels, and compared fixed logistic/boosting models with adaptation-only updates. Sealed both test prediction files before evaluation.

The challenger improved 2020 Brier by .0007118 (paired 95% interval [.0005154, .0009197] improvement), yet observed events were 6.2–8.3 times predicted across models. The resulting memo restricts probability use despite useful ranking. Separate 2023 transport, unresolved-outcome bounds, review-capacity diagnostics and broad-group uncertainty preserve the failure cases.

Business relevance: probability monitoring, credit-model validation, temporal drift, risk review allocation and model governance. This is historical US mortgage research, not a deployable Indian underwriting or IFRS 9 model.
